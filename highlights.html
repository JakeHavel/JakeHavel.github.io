Highlights

A few academic, technical, and workplace experiences that show how I learn and contribute.

01 · Academic

Stony Brook University Dean's List

Being named to the Dean's List for Fall 2023 and Spring 2025 recognizes my academic work as a Computer Science student at Stony Brook University. My studies have included computer security, web security, systems administration, systems fundamentals, and technical communications. These courses have strengthened the foundation I use to explore infrastructure and security, and they encourage me to connect theory with practical projects and professional experiences.

02 · Professional

IT Infrastructure Externship

During my July–August 2025 externship with Elite Computer Systems, I delivered on-site technical support and diagnosed networking issues across VMware and Windows environments. I configured Fortinet firewalls, managed Active Directory user accounts, and performed preventative system updates. The experience gave me practical exposure to client-facing infrastructure work and showed me how clear communication and routine maintenance contribute to reliable operations.

03 · Academic project

Secure Web Server Infrastructure

For an academic project, I configured a Linux virtual machine and deployed a LAMP stack to host a WordPress content management system. I added SSH public-key authentication, fail2ban, and self-signed SSL certificates for HTTPS, then automated daily web-server and database backups with cron. I also configured Exim4 for system-event notifications and used SSH port forwarding to reach restricted resources. The work brought system administration and security concepts together in a functioning environment.

04 · Work & community

Fundraising at Costco

Alongside my cashier responsibilities at Costco Wholesale, I took part in a company fundraising campaign for Cohen's Children's Hospital. I helped raise $2,589 and ranked among the top three part-time cashiers for donation collections. The result reflects consistent effort during customer interactions and reminds me that small, everyday conversations can contribute to a larger community effort. I continue to build customer-service and communication skills in a fast-paced setting.
