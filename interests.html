Interests

The questions that keep me exploring technology, from the network edge to the systems behind an application.

01 · Connectivity

Computer networking

I'm interested in the infrastructure that lets devices and people communicate across distances. Networking connects closely to my curiosity about how the internet works and to the practical work I encountered during my IT Infrastructure Externship, where I diagnosed networking issues and worked with Fortinet firewalls. I want to keep strengthening my understanding of protocols, traffic flow, and the design choices that make networks reliable and secure.

02 · Reliability

Systems administration

Systems administration appeals to me because dependable services are built through many careful decisions: configuration, permissions, updates, monitoring, and recovery plans. My coursework and externship have given me exposure to Windows, Linux, VMware, and Active Directory. I also enjoy seeing how small operational habits, such as maintaining backups and applying preventative updates, can help keep systems useful and reduce avoidable interruptions.

03 · Protection

Web and infrastructure security

Security interests me because protecting a system requires both technical controls and a clear understanding of how people use it. Courses in Computer Security Fundamentals and Web Security have helped me explore the subject, while my Linux web-server project gave me a chance to apply measures such as SSH key authentication, fail2ban, HTTPS, and automated backups. I want to keep learning how to identify weaknesses and make practical, responsible improvements.
